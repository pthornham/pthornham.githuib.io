Launching lockdown browser support Usage went from zero to over 10% in one term.

The problem

zyBooks added lockdown browser support to its assessment platform. Instructors had to know it existed, and trust it, before they would turn it on for their exams.

What I did

  • Ran the go-to-market research with the assessments product manager and the platform manager.
  • Wrote the messaging, and confirmed every technical claim with product before it went out.
  • Built awareness campaigns and short how-to videos so instructors could see it working.
  • Gave sales a persona guide showing who cares about which capability, and which pain point to lead with.

Result

Usage went from 0 to over 10% in about three months.

Taking labs and a new assessment platform to market 82% more contacts reached with onboarding, and 31 short product videos for sales and customers.

The problem

zyBooks launched labs content and a brand-new assessment platform. Reps needed to know who to approach about what, and customers needed to get started without waiting on onboarding calls.

What I did

  • Led the go-to-market research, launch and campaigns, working directly with the labs and assessments product managers and the platform manager.
  • Wrote the product messaging from stakeholder interviews, and co-wrote the customer FAQs with product and sales.
  • Mapped buyer personas to the capabilities each one cares about and the pain points reps should lead with.
  • Produced 31 product videos under four minutes each (17 for labs, 14 for assessment), with persona-focused versions on a private sales page and how-to versions on the public customer site.
  • Built self-service guides for both products.
  • Built two self-serve onboarding pages myself, writing the HTML and making step-by-step videos from past live sessions plus new material I wrote, recorded and edited. The platform team then built them into the product.
  • Run a recurring review with sales and product that reorders content around what customers are asking about now.

Result

Contacts who accessed onboarding in any form rose 82% from fall 2025 to fall 2026, and fewer paid, consultant-hosted live sessions were needed. Sales and customers hear the same story, each in the version built for them.

A renewal campaign that took work off sales $6M in sales impact.

The problem

Renewals depended on reps reaching each customer at the right moment, on top of everything else on their plates.

What I did

Built a campaign that used CRM data to reach customers at the critical points in their renewal cycle, and tracked engagement the whole way through. Renewal outreach moved from the reps to marketing automation.

Result

$6M in cumulative sales impact, and reps got that time back for new business.

Measuring what my launches actually did Attribution reporting that connects campaigns to pipeline and revenue.

The problem

Marketing had no way to connect a campaign to pipeline or revenue, so there was no way to know which launch work paid off.

What I did

  • Built marketing's first attribution reporting, matching new leads to opportunities opened within 90 days across hundreds of campaigns over three years.
  • Gave leadership a revenue view for monthly and quarterly reviews, and gave marketers a drill-down into every asset in a campaign.
  • Found and fixed a gap that was silently dropping deals from the reports when the contact on a deal changed.

Result

We learned which channels actually drive revenue, and turned a webinar link that the data proved out into standard practice.
