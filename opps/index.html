Building attribution from nothing Marketing's first view of the pipeline and revenue its campaigns create.

The problem

Marketing had no way to connect a campaign to pipeline or revenue.

What I did

  • Built attribution reporting that matches each new marketing-qualified lead to opportunities opened within 90 days, tracking open pipeline and closed revenue across hundreds of campaigns over three years.
  • Designed two views from one data source: a revenue view for leadership's monthly and quarterly reviews, and a drill-down that lets marketers see every asset in a campaign, from sends to revenue.
  • Rebuilt it from Power BI into an AI tool I made, which cleans changing source data automatically.
  • When revenue from self-started evaluations began disappearing from the reports, I traced the records and found that changing the contact on a deal silently removed it. A CRM change now ties credit to the campaign, so it survives contact changes.

Result

We learned which channels actually drive revenue: webinars won in some areas, and a plain personal email with a direct reply won in others. A link one colleague shared during webinars showed a strong return, so it became standard practice.

Moving the team off Marketo 358 emails and 109 smart lists migrated, with sales keeping every alert it relied on.

The problem

The company moved from Marketo to Salesforce Marketing Cloud Account Engagement. Converting 358 emails by hand, at about 10 minutes each, was roughly 60 hours the team did not have.

What I did

  • As platform owner, built an AI-assisted tool that previewed every Marketo email and converted it to import-ready HTML.
  • Directed the conversion of 109 smart lists across the team in five days, using a tracking checklist.
  • Rebuilt lead scoring and rep alerts with reps, regional managers and the sales director.
  • Managed IP warming, ran the first wave of campaigns on the new platform, and wrote the training the team builds from.

Result

About 60 hours of manual work saved, and no lost signals for sales.

A renewal campaign that took work off sales $6M in sales impact.

The problem

Renewals depended on reps reaching each customer at the right moment, on top of everything else on their plates.

What I did

Built a campaign that used CRM data to reach customers at the critical points in their renewal cycle, and tracked engagement the whole way through. Renewal outreach moved from the reps to marketing automation.

Result

$6M in cumulative sales impact, and reps got that time back for new business.

Personalizing email from CRM data Content matched to each instructor's courses, and timed to their academic calendar.

The problem

Instructors got the same message at the same time, whatever they taught and wherever they were in their term.

What I did

  • Built email programs that changed content based on the courses each instructor teaches.
  • Timed sends around each instructor's term start and finals dates.
  • Built a tool that reads reports from sales reps and writes subject lines specific to each instructor's course.

Result

Personalized outreach at scale, using data already in the CRM and no extra platform.
